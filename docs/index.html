Safety
When to call the doctor›
  • Call 911: trouble breathing, blue or gray lips, a seizure, or can't wake up
  • Go to the ER: a baby under 3 months with 100.4°F or higher, a stiff neck, or a rash that doesn't fade when pressed
  • Call the doctor: 104°F or higher (103°F for adults), a fever lasting more than 3 days, a fever that comes back, signs of dehydration (no pee in 8 hrs, no tears, can't keep fluids down), or ear or chest pain
  • Possible flu? Tamiflu works best when started within 48 hours.
  • Never give aspirin to kids. Skip cough medicine under age 6. Junior Strength chewables and infant drops are a different strength.
Tend helps you keep track. It's not medical advice. Check doses with your doctor or pharmacist.
